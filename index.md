# Privacy Policy for Room Measure Pro

**Effective Date:** January 10, 2026  
**Last Updated:** January 10, 2026

## Introduction

Room Measure Pro ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

**Account Information:** When you create a contractor profile, we collect:
- Company name
- Contact information (email, phone number, address)
- Business license number (optional)
- Zip code, city, and state for localized pricing
- Labor rates and tax rates

**Job and Project Data:** When you create jobs and estimates, we collect:
- Customer information (name, email, phone, address)
- Project photos
- Measurements and calculations
- Estimates and bids
- Job notes and descriptions

### Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:** We collect:
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Device identifiers (for crash reporting)

**Usage Data:** We collect:
- Features used within the App
- Calculators accessed
- Time spent in the App
- Error logs and crash reports

### Camera and Photos

The App requires camera access to:
- Take photos of job sites
- Measure dimensions from photos
- Generate before/after visualizations

Photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly use Pro features (AI estimate generation, before/after visualization).

### Location Data

We collect your zip code to provide localized material pricing. We do not track your precise GPS location.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- **Provide App functionality:** Enable measurements, calculations, estimates, and job management
- **Localized pricing:** Adjust material costs based on your region
- **AI-powered features:** Generate estimates and before/after visualizations (Pro users only)
- **PDF generation:** Create professional estimate documents
- **Email delivery:** Send estimates to customers on your behalf
- **Improve the App:** Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
- **Customer support:** Respond to your inquiries and troubleshoot issues
- **Legal compliance:** Comply with applicable laws and regulations

## Data Storage

**Local Storage:** Most of your data (jobs, measurements, photos, estimates) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you use AI-powered Pro features.

**Cloud Storage (Optional):** When you use Pro features that require AI processing (estimate generation, before/after visualization), photos and job descriptions are temporarily sent to our secure servers for processing. Processed data is immediately deleted after delivery to your device.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

**Service Providers:** We use third-party services to operate the App:
- **AI Processing:** OpenAI API for estimate generation and image processing (Pro features only)
- **Analytics:** Crash reporting and usage analytics (anonymized)
- **Email Delivery:** Email service providers when you send estimates to customers

**Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

**Business Transfers:** If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Your Rights and Choices

**Access and Update:** You can access and update your contractor profile information at any time through the Settings screen.

**Delete Data:** You can delete individual jobs, estimates, and photos at any time. To delete all data, uninstall the App.

**Opt-Out of Analytics:** You can disable crash reporting and analytics in the App settings (future feature).

**Email Communications:** You control when and to whom you send estimates. We do not send marketing emails.

## Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information:
- Local data is stored securely on your device
- Data transmitted to our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
- AI processing is performed on secure, SOC 2 compliant servers
- We do not store your photos or job data on our servers after processing

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

Room Measure Pro is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services (e.g., Home Depot, Lowe's for material reference). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

## In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase (Pro upgrade for $4.99). Payment processing is handled by Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle payment data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
